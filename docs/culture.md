# HR culture interview

Culture is assessed on behaviour, with the same rigour as code. HR runs a structured interview for every role, with the same questions and a [scored form](kit/culture-interview-form.md). We look for people who add to our culture, not people who resemble the panel.

| Behaviour | Core question | Strong evidence | Concern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ownership** | "Tell me about a commitment you missed. When did you realise, who did you tell, what happened next?" | Saw it early, told people, proposed recovery | Blames others; claims it never happens |
| **Candour** | "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a manager or client." | Disagreed with evidence, kept the relationship | Stayed silent, or fought to win |
| **Rigour** | "What standard do you hold yourself to that others sometimes don't?" | Concrete practice tied to real consequences | Slogans without examples |
| **Respect** | "Describe working with someone very different from you." | Adapted, learned, credits others | Dismissive language about colleagues |
| **Learning** | "What have you learned recently on your own, and where did you use it?" | Specific, recent, applied | Learns only when required |

## HR also checks
- Motivation: why us, why now, what they want in 2 years
- Expectations of their lead and team, matched against the Hiring Brief
- Fit with real working conditions: time zones, client exposure, intense periods
- Career moves, discussed as context and learning, never as a judgement
- For Tech Lead and Architect: how they give hard feedback and develop people

!!! warning "Guard rails"
    - Every culture concern cites a specific behaviour, just like a technical concern.
    - "Not a culture fit" alone is never a reason.
    - No questions on age, family plans, religion, ethnicity, health or politics.
    - Map these five behaviours to the company's published values once confirmed.
