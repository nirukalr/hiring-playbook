# Feedback and decisions

Every interview produces a written, scored record within 24 hours. Each interviewer completes the same [feedback form](kit/feedback-form.md) for every stage, alone, before seeing anyone else's. The debrief starts from these records, and the decision note is kept with them in the hiring system.

## The 4-point scale
- **1 · Clear gap:** serious concern at this level
- **2 · Below the bar:** leans no hire
- **3 · Meets the bar:** what we expect at this level
- **4 · Above the bar:** would raise our average
- **N/A · Not assessed:** never guess

## Competencies and anchors

| Competency | 2 · below the bar | 3 · meets the bar | 4 · above the bar |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Technical depth | Names tools, cannot explain them in own work | Explains own work down to data, code, config | Explains why the platform behaves as it does |
| Problem solving | Needs heavy prompting; ignores new facts | Breaks problems down; revises on new facts | Finds edge cases the panel had not raised |
| System design | Happy path only | Handles failure, consistency, observability | Clear options with cost and trade-offs |
| Code quality and testing | Tests are an afterthought | Tests business rules; readable code | Test strategy tied to risk |
| Ownership and delivery | Cannot separate own work from team's | Owned features end to end; raised issues early | Drove outcomes beyond their own task |
| Communication | Unclear or rambling | Clear, structured; clarifies requirements | Adapts to audience; calm in conflict |
| Learning and curiosity | Learns only when told | Recent self-driven learning, applied | Visible depth in self-driven work |
| Leadership and influence | Directs by authority or avoids conflict | Has grown people and steered decisions | Builds teams and shifts direction across groups |

## Decision rule
- Scores are read aloud before any discussion.
- **Hire:** no 1s, at least 3 on every must-have from every interviewer, weighted average 3 or more, and a yes on culture from HR.
- A 2 on a must-have is resolved with evidence (references, a follow-up, an exercise), never debate. Unresolved means no hire.
- The independent interviewer's and HR's concerns are answered in writing in the [decision note](kit/decision-note.md).
- "Not sure" is a no hire. Delivery pressure does not lower the bar; raise it with the CTO Office as a capacity issue instead.

Use the [feedback builder](feedback-builder.md) to apply each role's weights and produce text for the hiring system.
