# Engineering Hiring Playbook

<p class="page-meta">Version 1.0 · Responsible: CTO Office · Maintained by the Hiring Excellence Group</p>

One standard way to find, assess and hire engineers at Mitra AI, from interns to architects. The project that needs the person owns the decision, independent interviewers hold the company bar, and HR guards our culture.

## Every hire passes three lenses

| Lens | The question | Who guards it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Engineering bar** | Would they raise our average engineering quality, on any project? | Independent interviewer |
| **Project fit** | Can they deliver what this project needs in the next 6 to 12 months? | The project team that needs the person |
| **Culture** | Will they strengthen how we work: ownership, candour, rigour, respect and learning? | HR, with evidence from every interviewer |

!!! important "The rule"
    A hire needs a yes on all three. A brilliant engineer who would damage the team, or a good culture fit who cannot do the work, is a no.

## Principles every interviewer follows

- **Evidence over impressions.** Score what was said and done, not confidence.
- **Depth over keywords.** Start from real work and follow it to data, code, tests and operations.
- **Reasoning over first answers.** How someone revises under new facts matters more than being right first time.
- **Same structure, same bar.** Questions may vary; stages, competencies and scale do not.
- **Score alone, then discuss.** Feedback is submitted before anyone talks to the panel.
- **Each stage resolves a doubt.** Add an assignment or POC only to answer a named question.
- **Rigorous and respectful.** Probe hard, be candid about the job, and treat every candidate as a future colleague or customer.
- **Fair by design.** Protected attributes are never asked about or considered.
