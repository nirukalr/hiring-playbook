# Interviewer preparation

No one interviews without the project's context. Interviewers from outside a project test what they know, not what the project needs, unless we give them that context.

## Three things that fix it

1. **Certified pool.** Read this playbook, shadow 2 interviews scoring silently, lead 2 with an observer, then sign-off by the Hiring Excellence Group. Grouped by discipline and level. At most 2 interviews a week each.
2. **[Project Hiring Brief](../kit/project-hiring-brief.md).** Two to three sanitised pages from the hiring lead: the project, why the role exists, the first 90 days, the stack, the 3 hardest problems, team reality, must-haves, scenario and glossary.
3. **The 30-minute briefing.** Once per role, recorded for later interviewers.

!!! important "Readiness test"
    Before the first candidate, an interviewer from outside the project explains the role back in two minutes. If they cannot, the brief or the briefing is not ready.

## The 30-minute briefing
1. **The project and why this role** (5 min)
2. **The hard parts** (10 min): the 3 hardest problems, the architecture sketch, what a strong answer sounds like
3. **What we must not compromise on** (5 min): must-haves, deal-breakers, the reality of pace and client exposure
4. **The standard scenario** (5 min): probe points and expected answers
5. **Questions** (5 min): gaps found are added to the brief

## Preparation timeline

| When | What | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On assignment to the role | Read the Hiring Brief and the role track | 45 min |
| Before the first candidate | Attend or watch the briefing; add gaps to the brief | 30 min |
| Optional, long-running roles | Sit in on a standup or demo, or walk a sanitised architecture with the team | 60 min |
| 2 days before | Read the CV and AI screening notes; choose the deep-dive project and 3 probes | 20 min |
| 1 day before | Sync with the co-interviewer: who leads which block, who scores which competency | 10 min |
| Within 24 hours after | Submit the feedback form alone, with evidence | 20 min |
| After the debrief | Report what the brief was missing | 5 min |

About 3 hours for the first candidate on a role, then about 2 hours per candidate. TA sends each interviewer a candidate pack: CV, AI screening notes, Hiring Brief, stage guide and feedback form link.
