# Architecture presentation and kata

**Who uses it:** Architect · sent 1 week ahead

Use the copy button on the block below. The editable master copy lives in the shared drive: *add link*.

```text
ARCHITECTURE PORTFOLIO PRESENTATION  (60 min)
Present one or two architectures you shaped (sanitise client details).
For each, about 15 minutes:
  - Context: business problem, constraints, team, timeline
  - Options considered and why you chose this one
  - Key decisions: data, integration, security, cost, operations
  - What happened in production
  - What you regret, and what you'd change today
Then 30 minutes of questions from the panel.

ARCHITECTURE KATA  (90 min, separate session)
Brief handed out at the start; candidate designs live.
At 45 min the panel adds a constraint (e.g. data residency,
a 50% cost cut, a legacy system that cannot change).
```
