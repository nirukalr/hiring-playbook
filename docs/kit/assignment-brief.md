```text
ASSIGNMENT BRIEF
Role: ________  Size: Take-home (≤4 h) / Core case study (1–2 days) / Full (3–5 days)
Doubt this assignment must resolve: ______________________

The problem
  A fictional or sanitised scenario in the project's domain.
  State the business rules, and leave some ambiguity on purpose.

What to deliver
  - Repository shared with ________ at the start
  - Frequent commits (we look at how the work evolved)
  - README: how to run, test and (if relevant) deploy
  - QUESTIONS_AND_ASSUMPTIONS.md: every assumption, its source, its impact
  - Tests for the business rules

Rules
  - Effort estimate: ____ hours. Deadline: ____ (at least 7 days for case studies)
  - Questions to ________; replies within 1 working day
  - AI tools are allowed; you must explain and change any part of your work
  - No secrets, credentials or .env files in the repository

What happens next
  - Review by two engineers by ________
  - 60-min walkthrough: one business rule and its test, one design choice
    and the alternative, a small live change, what you'd do for production

Scoring (adapt weights)
  Correctness 25 · Testing 20 · Design and code 20 · Cloud/ops 15 ·
  API docs 10 · Assumptions 5 · README 5
  Plus process: questions asked, commit history, early risk raising
```
