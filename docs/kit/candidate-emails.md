# Candidate emails

**Who uses it:** TA and hiring lead

Use the copy button on the block below. The editable master copy lives in the shared drive: *add link*.

```text
1) INVITATION TO TECHNICAL INTERVIEW
Subject: Your technical interview with Mitra AI
Hi [Name],
Thank you for your time so far. Your technical interview is on [date, time,
time zone] for 75 minutes, with [interviewers]. We will spend most of the
time on something you have built, then explore a few "what if" changes,
a short design problem and a small coding task. We will ask follow-up
questions to understand your thinking, and "I don't know" is a fine answer.
Please have a camera and a quiet space. Link: [link]
Regards, [Name]

2) ASSIGNMENT BRIEFING
Use the assignment brief template. Always include: effort estimate,
deadline, named contact and reply time, AI policy, and the review date.

3) DECISION AFTER INTERVIEW — NOT PROCEEDING
Subject: Your application for [role]
Hi [Name],
Thank you for the time you put into our process. We have decided not to
move forward this time. The main reason was [one specific, kind reason,
e.g. "we needed more depth in designing for failure in distributed
systems"]. We enjoyed [one genuine positive]. We would welcome an
application again in the future.
Regards, [Name]
```
