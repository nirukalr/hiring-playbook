# Leadership scenario

**Who uses it:** Tech Lead · sent 48 hours ahead

Use the copy button on the block below. The editable master copy lives in the shared drive: *add link*.

```text
LEADERSHIP SCENARIO  (candidate presents 20 min, discussion 40 min)
You join a 6-person team as tech lead. The release is 4 weeks away.
  - Velocity has dropped for three sprints; estimates are often wrong
  - One senior engineer is disengaged and openly critical
  - The client added two "must-have" features last week
  - Production has had two incidents this month, both from rushed fixes

Prepare a plan for your first two weeks:
  1. What you do in your first 5 days, and why
  2. What you tell the client, and when
  3. How you handle the senior engineer
  4. What you cut, protect or change in the process

Panel probes
  "What if the client refuses to move the date?"
  "Tell me about a real time you did something like this."
  "What would tell you after week 2 that the plan is failing?"
```
