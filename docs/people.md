# People and responsibilities

The project lives with the result, so the project carries the main responsibility. Independent interviewers protect the company bar, HR protects culture and fairness, and the Hiring Excellence Group keeps the system current.

## The project team: owns project fit and the decision
- **Hiring lead** (tech lead or delivery lead): writes the Project Hiring Brief, runs the briefing, chairs the debrief, signs the decision note.
- **Project engineers** (1 or 2): build the realistic scenario and exercise, run the team interview, review assignments.
- **Delivery manager** (Tech Lead and Architect roles): joins the stakeholder interview.
- **Onboarding buddy:** receives the risk areas and runs the first 90 days.
- **Accountable for** the hire's 90-day success and time to fill.

## HR: owns culture, fairness and candidate experience
- Runs the structured [culture interview](culture.md) for every role.
- Checks motivation, expectations, stability in context, and references.
- Checks fairness: same structure, no protected attributes, reasons recorded.
- Owns candidate communication standards and the offer.
- Can block a hire on a culture concern backed by evidence; resolved at the debrief.

## Independent interviewer: holds the company bar
- Comes from the certified pool, outside the project.
- Matched on discipline, at or above the role's level.
- Prepares from the Hiring Brief and the briefing.
- Leads the what-if challenge; scores engineering-bar competencies first.
- Their concerns must be answered in writing in the decision note.

## Talent acquisition: runs the logistics
- Sourcing, AI CV screening, scheduling and candidate packs.
- Chases feedback until every form is in within 24 hours.
- Keeps candidates informed within 2 working days at every stage.
- Reports hiring metrics to the Hiring Excellence Group monthly.
