# Stage details

## 1. Role kickoff
- **Owner:** project hiring lead (tech lead or delivery lead), with TA.
- **Do:** choose the role track; set must-haves, nice-to-haves and deal-breakers; write the [Project Hiring Brief](../kit/project-hiring-brief.md); decide whether an assignment or POC applies.
- **Exit:** someone outside the project reads the brief and explains the role back in two minutes.

## 2. Panel and briefing
- **Owner:** hiring lead.
- **Do:** name the panel, including an independent interviewer from the certified pool matched on discipline and level. Run the 30-minute [briefing](../interviewers/preparation.md#the-30-minute-briefing) and record it.
- **Exit:** every panel member has read the brief and asked their questions.

## 3. CV screen
- **Owner:** TA runs the AI screening prompt; the hiring lead or a delegate reviews.
- **Do:** score against the role rubric; mark evidenced, inferred and needs-validation items. A human reads every CV the AI would reject within 10 points of the threshold.
- **Exit:** every applicant hears back within 2 working days.

## 4. HR screen (30 min)
- **Owner:** TA or HR.
- **Do:** check motivation, notice period, salary range, location and hours, spoken English. Explain every stage, the timeline and the in-depth interview style.

## 5. Technical interview
- **Owner:** hiring lead and independent interviewer.
- **Do:** follow the [standard structure](../interviewers/conducting.md). Each interviewer submits feedback alone within 24 hours.
- **Exit:** both score 3 or more on every must-have, or the panel writes down one specific doubt to resolve.

## 6. Evaluation exercise
- **Owner:** project engineers review; hiring lead decides the method.
- **Do:** use the role's primary method from [Evaluation methods](../methods.md). Add a heavier method only for a written doubt.

## 7. Team interview
- **Owner:** the project team.
- **Do:** the people who will work with the hire meet them on a realistic problem, and the candidate meets the team they would join.

## 8. HR culture interview
- **Owner:** HR partner.
- **Do:** run the [structured culture interview](../culture.md) and complete the scored form.

## 9. Debrief and decision (30 min)
- **Owner:** hiring lead chairs.
- **Do:** follow the [debrief agenda](../kit/decision-note.md); apply the [decision rule](../feedback.md#decision-rule).
- **Exit:** a signed decision note within 2 working days of the last stage.

## 10. Offer and 90 days
- **Owner:** TA and hiring lead.
- **Do:** references focused on needs-validation items; offer; the hiring lead calls the candidate. On day one, hand risk areas to the onboarding buddy. At 90 days, score the hire on the same scorecard and send it to the Hiring Excellence Group.
