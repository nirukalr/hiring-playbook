# Role tracks

All tracks share the competencies, the 4-point scale, the [feedback form](../kit/feedback-form.md) and the [decision rule](../feedback.md#decision-rule). Each track sets its own weights, must-haves, stage formats, evaluation method and panel.

## Competency weights (out of 100)

Bold values are must-haves.

| Competency | Intern | SE | SSE | Tech Lead | Architect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Technical depth | 10 | **20** | **20** | 15 | **15** |
| Problem solving and reasoning | **25** | 20 | 15 | 10 | 10 |
| System design and architecture | – | 10 | **15** | **15** | **30** |
| Code quality and testing | 15 | **20** | 15 | 5 | 5 |
| Ownership and delivery | 10 | **15** | **15** | **20** | 10 |
| Communication and collaboration | 15 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| Learning and curiosity | **25** | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| Leadership and influence | – | – | 5 | **20** | **15** |

The hiring lead may move up to 10 points between competencies to fit the project, and records the change in the Project Hiring Brief.

## At a glance

| | Intern | SE | SSE | Tech Lead | Architect |

| We assess | Potential: fundamentals, reasoning, learning speed | Ships tested features end to end | Complex features, failure handling, mentoring | Technical outcome of a team; recovers delivery | Solutions across projects; technical risk |
| Primary method | Online coding test + live coding | Pair programming or take-home | System design + code review | Design + code review; leadership scenario | Portfolio presentation + architecture kata |
