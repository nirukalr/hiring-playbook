# Tech Lead

Owns the technical outcome of a team, builds the team, and is the client's technical counterpart. For a new project, hire the lead first.

**Candidate time:** about 6 hours · **Decision:** within 28 days

## What we look for
- Still technically strong: can design, review and unblock
- Has recovered a slipping delivery and can say how
- Builds teams: hiring, mentoring, giving hard feedback
- Handles client disagreement with evidence and calm

**Must-haves:** system design and architecture; ownership and delivery; leadership and influence.

## Panel
Delivery head or engineering manager (hiring lead), one independent from the Tech Leads' Forum, one from the Architects' Forum for design, and future team members.

## Stages
| Stage | Format | Time | Who |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Technical interview | Standard 75-min structure on a system they led | 75 min | Hiring lead + TL Forum independent |
| System design and code review | Design exercise (45) plus reviewing a pull request as a lead would (30) | 75 min | Architects' Forum member |
| Leadership scenario | A [delivery-recovery case](../kit/leadership-scenario.md) sent 48 h ahead; candidate presents (20) and is challenged (40) | 60 min | Hiring lead + delivery manager |
| Team and stakeholder interview | Engineers who would report to them, plus a product owner or delivery manager | 60 min | Project team |
| HR culture interview | Structured values interview with a leadership-behaviour focus | 45 min | HR |
| Head of engineering conversation | Expectations, career, sell the role | 30 min | Optional |

## Sample questions
- "Tell me about a delivery that was slipping. When did you know, who did you tell, and what did you change?"
- "An engineer on your team keeps missing estimates. Walk me through your first conversation."
- "The client wants a feature you believe will hurt the system. What do you do?"
- "How did you hire for your last team? Who was your best hire, and why?"

## Red flags
- "We" for everything; no personal decisions
- Blames the team or the client for every miss
- Can no longer go deep technically
