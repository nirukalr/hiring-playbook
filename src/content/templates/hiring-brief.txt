PROJECT HIRING BRIEF  (2–3 pages, no confidential client data)
Role: ____________   Level: Intern / SE / SSE / TL / Architect
Hiring lead: ____________   Date: ____________

1. The project in one paragraph
   Domain, users, business goal, stage (new build / scale-up / support).

2. Why this role exists
   The gap it fills. What goes wrong if it stays empty.

3. The first 90 days
   3–5 concrete things the hire will do or own.

4. Tech stack and architecture
   Languages, frameworks, cloud services, data stores.
   Attach a simple, sanitised architecture sketch.

5. The three hardest problems
   e.g. money-handling rules, multi-tenancy, legacy integration.
   These become deep-dive and what-if material.

6. Team and ways of working
   Team size and roles, time zones, client exposure, rituals,
   on-call, pace and known intense periods.

7. Must-haves / nice-to-haves / deal-breakers
   One line each, plus how to recognise it in an interview.

8. Competency weights and must-haves (from the role track)

9. Standard scenario and coding check
   The problem every candidate gets, probe points,
   what a 3 and a 4 answer sound like.

10. Who will thrive here (and who would struggle)

11. Glossary (10–15 domain and project terms)

12. Why a strong engineer would want this role
